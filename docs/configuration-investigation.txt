Confirmed observations
- Applying Continuous configuration sends one bare ASCII C; the first two observed instances received C<CR>.
- Selecting Incremental initially sends bare I, receives I<CR>, then about 0.60 seconds later sends MI020<CR>.
- MI020<CR> is six bytes: 4d 49 30 32 30 0d.
- The wire parameter string is associated with the operator's Incremental Force 20 setting. Interpretation as an increment-setting command is well supported by context, but only one numeric value was actually transmitted.
- No numeric global-maximum-force value was sent during either 450/500 apply action. Both only sent C.
- Contrary to the intended experiment, neither MI025 nor MI026 was sent. Only additional I bytes were transmitted.
- After frame 88 there are no captured incoming serial bytes, including after the final C.
- Host USB write completions were visible. USB delivery does not prove firmware acceptance or a successful mode change.
- No T serial command was transmitted in this capture.

Interpretations and limits
The global force limit appears to be maintained/enforced in the PC application rather than sent on configuration apply. This remains an inference; it could be transmitted during another unobserved operation.
C and I are strongly associated with Continuous/Incremental selection. H/U/H timing during the observed automatic home supports an initialization/homing exchange, but does not isolate each letter's physical effect.
The absence of replies after MI020 prevents concluding that reselecting the same mode alone causes the problem. The device may be awaiting further input, the app/firmware sequence may be incomplete, or another issue may have occurred. A firmware hang and a parser state have not been proven.
The final C was sent but not acknowledged. The GUI was restored to Continuous / Global Max 500 / stored Incremental Force 20 according to the operator. The tester's internal mode was not confirmed.
Do not describe MI025 or MI026 as verified commands; they were never observed on the wire. Do not use the final C as proof of hardware state.

Next investigation
Preserve this capture before attempting recovery. Re-establish an acknowledged known state through the original software with the operator prepared for automatic homing, before any further machine operation.
For a future diagnostic console, support raw receive logging, explicit per-message transmission, exact byte/CR visibility, and no automatic alphabet sweep. Do not guess a Stop command.
A controlled new session could isolate mode selection from parameter setting, but should not blindly replay MI020 or additional data to a potentially pending parser. Stop/abort and cyclic commands remain unknown.
